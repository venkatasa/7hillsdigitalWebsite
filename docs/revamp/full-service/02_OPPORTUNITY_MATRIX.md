**What this tells us**

1. **Buyers search for *who*, not *what*.** "IT company near me" is large and growing,
   while "managed IT services" is barely searched. Managed IT is bought through local
   search, referrals and Google Business Profile, not a keyword page.
2. **The large technical terms are not buying signals.** Microsoft 365, Azure, AWS,
   cyber security and IT support are dominated by logins, courses and jobs. They are
   poor SEO targets, but the services can still be strong *sellers*. Treat them as
   conversion pages, not acquisition pages.
3. **Business-application nouns convert.** CRM, accounting software, booking system
   and ERP keep their commercial intent (the earlier, replicated runs).
4. **SharePoint is the one Microsoft term that is rising** (+19%).

### D. Public procurement [S]

SITA's transversal ICT services contract (RFB 1183) accredits suppliers per category:
ICT management · business planning and development · **business solutions delivery** ·
**information security** · business solution implementation · **applications
development** · ICT services support management · data centre · communication network
· LAN and desktop · planning and organisation (ITWeb, 10/02/2025). eTenders listings
are tagged *software development, managed services, support and maintenance*.

**Implication:** our catalogue maps cleanly to the categories procurement uses, so
naming services the same way helps. Public-sector work needs SITA accreditation, CSD
registration and B-BBEE, and the sales cycles are long. **Recommended as a later
channel, not a launch target.** CSD registration is free and worth doing now (owner
action).

### E. Competitor positioning [S]: observed, not audited

| Pattern | Example | Lesson |
|---|---|---|

| Giants own enterprise outsourcing | BCX, NTT DATA, Altron, IBM, Accenture: just over 45% (Mordor) | Compete on SME and mid-market projects, not on outsourcing |

\* **Hosting can be sold only once R1 to R3 (backups, database exposure, debug mode)

from a supplier whose own production site shows a debug page is a story that ends
the business.

## 2.3 Recommended priority

**Tier 1: lead with these now.** Evidence exists; we can deliver tomorrow.
1. Custom software and web application development
2. Business applications: CRM systems, customer and employee portals, booking and
   scheduling, accounting and invoicing integrations
3. Process automation, workflow and approvals, API integration
4. Websites and industry platforms (existing, unchanged)
5. Managed hosting and maintenance (**after R1–R3**)

**Tier 2: highest commercial upside, published only when the owner confirms
capability.** These are also the recurring-revenue engines.
6. Managed IT support (per-user monthly)
7. Microsoft 365 consulting, administration, migration and governance; SharePoint;
   Power Automate
8. Microsoft 365 security, identity and access, MFA
9. AI document processing and business assistants

**Tier 3: listed in the catalogue as "on request / with a specialist partner", no
dedicated page yet.** Cloud migration (Azure/AWS), security assessments, BI, mobile,
ERP, DR, cloud cost optimisation, infrastructure monitoring.

### The commercial logic

Tier 1 wins the **first project**. Tier 2 turns a project client into a **monthly
contract** (support, Microsoft 365, hosting). The platforms remain the **low-risk
entry point** for small businesses. The fastest route to recurring revenue is
**confirming Tier 2 capability**, not building more pages.
